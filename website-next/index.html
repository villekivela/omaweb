Navigation

Go anywhere,
faster.

Press o to go somewhere new, f to follow a link, and J and K to move between tabs. Pinned tabs sit at the top of the sidebar, today’s tabs run underneath, and each Space keeps its own.

The window answers the same keys: try Ctrl+B, Ctrl+Y or T.

No sidebar Ctrl+B

The page gets the whole window.

Hide the sidebar and nothing is left but the page. Your tabs are still a key away: J and K keep moving between them, and Ctrl+B brings the sidebar back.

History Ctrl+Y

Every Space remembers on its own.

History opens over the page and searches only the Space you are in. Delete one visit, a whole site, a stretch of time or the lot, and the other Spaces never notice.

Settings Ctrl+,

Settings, next to your tabs.

Settings opens in the same window, beside the tabs it changes. Every key binding lives in one keybindings.json, so any command can move to the key you want it on.

Content blocking

Blocking, built in.

EasyList and EasyPrivacy are on from the first run, with cosmetic rules and a count of what was blocked. Add lists, write your own rules, or turn blocking off for one site.
