Install Omaweb

Keyboard first. Private. Open. For people who know where they’re going.

  • One field for everything

    Type a few letters and the Omnibar finds the page: an open tab in this Space or another, a visit from history, a search with your engine’s suggestions. Every browser command runs from the same field. o

  • Work and personal, kept apart

    Each Space keeps its own logins, history and tabs in the same window, and wears its own colour from your theme. J and K move through its tabs.

  • Keyboard first

    Link hints on f, Vim-style keys, and every binding yours to change in keybindings.json. The mouse is optional.

  • Follows your Omarchy theme

    Switch your Omarchy theme and Omaweb switches with it: its colours, its fonts, its opacity, and the road on every new tab. This page follows the theme you pick here. T

  • Private by default

    No telemetry, and no account to make. Every request the browser makes on its own is written down.

  • Blocking, built in

    EasyList and EasyPrivacy from the first run, with cosmetic rules and a count of what was blocked. Add lists, write your own rules, or turn blocking off for one site.

  • For pros

    Two pages side by side in split view, and DevTools docked beside the page.

  • Agents drive their own Spaces

    An Agent works in a Space of its own, marked in the footer, and never in yours unless you let it. How Agents work

Get on the road.

Omaweb ships from its own pacman repository, so it installs and upgrades with the rest of your system. Two steps, once.

The fingerprint is the one thing to check. It comes from here, not from a keyserver, and pacman installs nothing that key did not sign. From then on, sudo pacman -Syu keeps it current. A single package or a build from source instead? The README has both.
