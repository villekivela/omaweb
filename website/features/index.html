Features

What is in the browser.

Everything here works today, on the release you can download, unless the item says otherwise. The landing page says why the browser is the way it is; this is what is actually in it.

  • Spaces

    Separate browsing identities in one window, each with its own cookies, logins, history and tabs. Work and personal stay signed in side by side.

  • Split view

    Two tabs side by side where one page would be. The active one keeps the address bar and the commands; a split comes back after a restart.

  • The Omnibar

    One overlay for everything: type an address, search the web, jump to a tab or a Space, or run any browser command.

  • Keyboard first

    Every binding is yours to change in keybindings.json. Link hints reach anything on a page, and the shortcut sheet is generated from the keymap you are running.

  • Private by default

    No browser account, no telemetry, nothing sent anywhere. Content blocking is built in, with EasyList, EasyPrivacy, cosmetic rules and scriptlets, and your own rules in uBlock Origin’s syntax.

  • Follows your theme

    Omaweb takes its colours from the Omarchy theme you are running and changes with it, without a restart.
